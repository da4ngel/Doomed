Maelis Harrowick the Pale is depicted as a pale, long-haired gothic figure accompanied by a white barn owl perched on their shoulder, set against a ruined cityscape. The figure wears a tattered black cloak fastened with a skull clasp, alongside elements such as metal chains, a pendant with dangling ornament, and tattered red and grey robes.
