Gloamreach is a core location on The Pale Coast, ruled by House Morvain, and has a garrison strength of 2483. It was founded in 246 AS and is characterized as quarantined and ominous.

[cite_1447ad9a8958d59d] [cite_2ed124bbce0f1b65] [cite_5d6cb2e4dbabbe50] [cite_578a359c3a424c0f]

The Cinder-Wrought Aegis was forged at Embermarch and is housed in Gloamreach. It was forged in 342 AS.
